Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

In short: Chinly works on your iPhone. Your head movements, posture history and settings stay on your phone. We have no accounts, no ads and no tracking across apps. What leaves your phone is what's needed to run your subscription and, unless you switch it off, anonymous counts of which screens and buttons are used.

Who we are

Chinly is made by Guillaume Rosenthal Salman, an independent developer in Spain, who is responsible for your data under the EU's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact: chinlyapp@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

  • Head movement from your AirPods. Chinly reads the angle of your head through iPhone's Motion & Fitness access to notice when you slouch. It's used in the moment and never stored or uploaded.
  • Your activity (walking, running, cycling, driving). Only used to pause alerts while you move, on your phone.
  • Your posture history. Time tracked, time slouched, how many alerts and your streak, saved on your phone to show your progress and write your recaps.
  • Your settings, such as your sound, strictness and "sitting tall" position.
  • Notifications. Recaps, reports and alerts are created on your phone. No server sends them.

We can't see any of this. Deleting the app deletes it.

What leaves your phone

Your subscription

Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your card or your Apple ID. To know whether your subscription is active, Chinly uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). RevenueCat receives a random ID made for the app, your purchases and their status, your country and store, and your device's app and system version. We use this only to give you what you paid for (the legal basis is our contract with you). RevenueCat's policy: revenuecat.com/privacy. Transfers to the USA are covered by the EU's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which parts of Chinly help and where people get stuck, the app sends simple events to PostHog (PostHog Inc.), stored on its servers in the EU (Frankfurt). For example: which setup screen was reached, the answer to "Where did you hear about us?", that tracking started or stopped and for how long, how many alerts a session had, that a break was taken, that a plan was chosen. Each event carries a random ID made for the app, the app and system version, the phone model and the language. No location, and we don't keep your IP address. It never includes your head angle, your posture history, your name, email or Apple ID, and it isn't used for ads or shared. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in improving the app. You can switch it off anytime in Settings > Share anonymous usage. PostHog's policy: posthog.com/privacy.

When you write to us

If you email us, we use your email and what you send only to answer you, and delete it when it's no longer needed.

Ratings

If you rate Chinly, Apple's own rating window is used. Apple handles it under its privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No ads, no selling or sharing of data, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No camera, no microphone, no location.
  • No selling of data and no ad tracking. The anonymous usage statistics above can be switched off in Settings.

Children

Chinly isn't made for children under 14 and we don't knowingly collect anything from them.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or move your data, or object to how it's used, by emailing chinlyapp@gmail.com. Most of your data is only on your phone: delete the app to erase it. For your subscription data at RevenueCat, write to us and we'll have it deleted (Apple keeps its own purchase records). You can also complain to Spain's data protection authority, the AEPD (aepd.es).

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Big changes will also be shown in the app.
