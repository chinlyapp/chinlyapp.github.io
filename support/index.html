Help

Questions, ideas or a problem? Email chinlyapp@gmail.com. A real person answers, usually within a day.

Common questions

Which AirPods work?

AirPods Pro, AirPods 3, AirPods 4 and AirPods Max. They need to be connected to your iPhone and in your ears.

Does it work with my phone locked?

Yes. Start tracking, lock your phone, and Chinly keeps listening for slouches. It plays a short sound in your AirPods, over your music.

Tracking stopped by itself

iPhone sometimes closes apps in the background, for example when memory is low. Chinly tells you when that happens (Settings > Notifications > If tracking stops). Open it and tap Start tracking again.

It catches me too often, or not enough

In Settings, change Strictness or Wait before alerting. If it seems off, set your sitting tall again (Settings > Sitting tall).

Alerts on my watch

Turn on Settings > Notifications > Slouch alerts. Apple Watch shows them by itself while your iPhone is locked. Fitbit, Garmin and other watches: allow Chinly in their app's notification settings. It may be listed by its app ID instead of its name.

Cancel or change my plan

On your iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Chinly. Or in Chinly: Settings > Manage subscription.

New phone, or I reinstalled

Open Chinly and tap Restore purchases (in Settings, or under the button on the plans screen), with the same Apple ID you subscribed with. Your posture history stays on your old phone; it isn't uploaded anywhere.

Delete my data

Delete the app. Everything Chinly saved is on your phone and goes with it. See the privacy policy for your subscription data.
